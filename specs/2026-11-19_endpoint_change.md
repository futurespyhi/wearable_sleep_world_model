# Binary endpoint-change diagnostic

Run on the fixed 38 nights from recording-time report `5af00c4`. The event equals one when the expert stage at the last completed epoch and the stage at the forecast endpoint differ. An intervening transition followed by a return to the original stage counts as zero. At +5, +15 and +30 minutes use the parent lead-specific mask requiring both stages known. Keep the seven test participants sealed.

Fit three separate binary logistic regressions per lead on identical eligible training origins. Model A uses four indicators for hours between recStart and cutoff, with cut points at 2, 4 and 6 hours. B adds the four saved-estimator probabilities of the current stage. For the 12 training participants generate these probabilities leaving each person's nights out of both standardization and the current-stage classifier. Use the original saved probabilities for validation. Model C adds four summaries from the history strictly before cutoff. For HR and wrist movement use respectively the epoch HR mean and the cached RMS of successive accelerometer vector differences. Use the mean over available epochs in the final five minutes and subtract the mean in the preceding 25 minutes to obtain the change. Include the recent level and change of each signal. Respect the cached validity masks. Impute missing summary values using training data.

Use mean binary log loss over eligible training origins with a fixed 0.01 L2 penalty coefficient and an unpenalized intercept for A, B and C. Leave time indicators on their 0/1 scale. Use training rows at each lead to standardize continuous predictors. Keep the true validation stages out of the predictors.

Evaluate binary NLL and binary Brier (squared error of change probability), averaged within participant and then equally across participants. Report observed same/changed counts, all arm scores, paired changes and leave-one-validation-person-out sensitivity. Keep fits fixed when omitting a validation participant. Lower scores favor a model. A stable gain requires C to beat both A and B on both metrics in the full evaluation and after each of the seven omissions, using unrounded scores. Primary lead is +30 minutes. Consider later four-stage integration only for a stable +30-minute gain. If improvement is only at +5 minutes, limit the finding to that horizon.
