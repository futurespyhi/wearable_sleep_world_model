# Transition controls on the fixed 38-night cohort

Use the audited cache and saved predictions from the [expanded comparison](../reports/20261119-stage-expanded/report.md) at commit `0e0c0a1127805b7fa1b7cca516ae686638207c12`. Preserve the same 969 validation origins and 1,936 training origins. The sealed test plays no role. Stage order is Wake, Light, Deep, REM.

- **Persistence.** Use the saved current-stage probability vector from learned persistence at each validation cutoff. Repeat it at all 60 horizons.
- **Prior mixture.** For each horizon predict `(1 − alpha_h) p_t + alpha_h prior_h`. Fit the 60 scalar coefficients on out-of-fold predictions within the 12 training participants. Hold out both nights of one participant at a time and fit the scaler, persistence classifier and lead prior using the other participants. Choose each weight in [0, 1] to minimize held-out NLL with equal weight per evaluable participant. Apply these weights to the saved validation probabilities and full training priors.
- **Stage transition.** For each horizon estimate a separate four-by-four matrix from retained training origins with known expert current and target labels. Current is the last completed epoch and the target is the horizon's future epoch. Normalize empirical counts within each current-stage row and use the full training prior for a row with no valid pairs. Predict `p_t @ T_h`. Use the three direct predictors as saved and average their scores across seeds.
- **Criterion.** At +30 minutes, transition must have strictly lower participant-balanced NLL than both prior mixture and stage prior on all known future labels. It must also have lower NLL than the prior mixture on the subgroup where known current and future reference stages differ. This subgroup concerns the two endpoint stages. A trajectory that changes and returns to its initial stage does not enter it.
- **Reporting.** Report NLL, four-stage macro-F1, Wake recall and target support at +5/+15/+30 minutes with the original participant-balanced metrics. Unknown current is permitted for overall scores and excluded from the subgroup. Group membership is assigned after predictions are frozen.
